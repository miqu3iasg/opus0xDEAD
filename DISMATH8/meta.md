# CS10226 — Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications

## Overview

**Author:** Kenneth H. Rosen
**Edition:** 8th Edition
**Subject:** Discrete Mathematics

_Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications_ provides a comprehensive foundation in discrete mathematics with applications to computer science, mathematics, and engineering.

The book develops formal reasoning and mathematical techniques used throughout computer science, covering logic and proofs, sets and functions, algorithms, number theory, induction and recursion, counting, probability, relations, graphs, trees, Boolean algebra, and models of computation.

## Learning Objectives

By completing this book, I should be able to:

- Reason formally using propositional and predicate logic.
- Construct and evaluate mathematical proofs.
- Work with sets, functions, sequences, summations, and matrices.
- Analyze algorithms and their asymptotic complexity.
- Apply number theory and modular arithmetic to computational problems.
- Understand cryptographic techniques based on number theory.
- Use mathematical induction, strong induction, and recursive reasoning.
- Define and analyze recursive algorithms and establish program correctness.
- Solve combinatorial counting problems.
- Apply discrete probability and expected value to computational problems.
- Solve recurrence relations and analyze divide-and-conquer algorithms.
- Work with relations, equivalence relations, and partial orderings.
- Model problems using graphs and analyze their properties.
- Work with trees, spanning trees, and minimum spanning trees.
- Understand Boolean functions, logic gates, and circuit minimization.
- Understand formal models of computation, including finite-state machines and Turing machines.
